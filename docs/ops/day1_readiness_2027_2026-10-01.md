## 3. Seed-count study for the daily chain

Data: the adopted served stack at full size, `results/engine_v0/v3full_COMB9GCTKD_s200_o0` (5,710 games x 200 seeds, the 02:06 adoption read), joined to the harness's close lines (`reference.load_lines(2025)`): 5,383 games with a close spread, 5,381 with a total. No new sim. Script: `scripts/diag_daily_seedcount_v1.py`; tables `results/seedcount_study.json` / `.csv` (gitignored). Method: the 200 seeds are cut into disjoint blocks of k (independent k-seed runs, since RNG is keyed on seed); per game and quantity the probability is estimated per block. Quantities: P(home wins), P(home covers the close spread, pushes half), P(total goes over the close total).

Run-to-run movement of one published probability (RMS over games, percentage points; empirical SD across blocks agrees with the binomial standard error to within 3%, so the 1/sqrt(k) law is confirmed and extrapolated beyond 100):

| seeds k | win SE | cover SE | over SE | cover: 95th pct of \|run A - run B\| | cover: share of games within 1 pp | cover: lean flips between two runs | cover: "edge >= 3 pp" status flips |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 10 | 13.5 | 15.4 | 15.3 | 40.0 | 18% | 39% | 32% |
| 25 | 8.5 | 9.8 | 9.6 | 28.0 | 12% | 33% | 33% |
| 50 | 6.0 | 6.9 | 6.8 | 20.0 | 8% | 28% | 29% |
| 100 | 4.3 | 4.9 | 4.8 | 14.0 | 7% | 22% | 26% |
| 200 (extrapolated, 1/sqrt k) | 3.0 | 3.5 | 3.4 | | | | |

(k = 5 is on a 20-point grid and is not informative; "within 1 pp" at k <= 10 is a grid artefact.)

Seed counts needed (binomial law, per-game p from the 200-seed run; "worst game" is p = 0.5, which is where a spread or total read lives: 84% of games have a cover probability in 0.35-0.65 and 74% an over probability there; only 37% of win probabilities do):

| target | win (RMS) | cover (RMS) | over (RMS) | any game incl. p = 0.5 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| movement < 1 pp | 1,823 | 2,382 | 2,327 | 2,500 |
| movement < 0.5 pp | 7,292 | 9,527 | 9,307 | 10,000 |

In points (what the ATS and O/U disagreement buckets read; within-game SD of margin 12.2 pts, of total 15.9 pts): SE of the sim mean margin is 0.86 pt at 200 seeds, 0.55 at 500, 0.39 at 1,000, 0.27 at 2,000; of the mean total 1.12 / 0.71 / 0.50 / 0.35. SE under 0.5 pt needs 597 seeds (margin) and 1,007 (total); under 0.25 pt needs 2,388 and 4,026.

What this means for the chain:
- At the chain default of 200 seeds a cover or over probability moves about 3.5 pp (RMS) between two runs, and a game's lean flips between two 100-seed runs 22% of the time (cover) and 18% (over). A bucketed edge table built on probabilities at that noise level misclassifies games into buckets, and selecting on a large edge selects positive noise (winner's-curse inflation of the apparent edge). This is why the minimum must come before any ROI number is read.
- PROPOSED minimum, for the PM to pre-register: 2,500 seeds per game for any published probability or ROI read (movement under 1 pp for every game including p = 0.5); the 0.5 pp level (10,000 seeds) is shown for reference. Edge buckets read on mean margin / total disagreement are adequate at 1,000 seeds (SE under 0.5 pt on both). Below the minimum the chain's outputs are plumbing and bias monitoring only and are labelled "not for ROI".
- Cost, from the measured engine rate (about 0.15 s per game-seed on one core, replay logs: 592 game-seeds in 118.7 s including a 30 s build): 2,500 seeds x 118 games = 44,000 core-seconds = 12.3 core-hours, about 37 minutes on 20 idle cores per pass; a 150-game day about 47 minutes. Not feasible on a box shared with research lanes, and two passes double it. This is a box / AWS-schedule decision for the PM.
- A smoother estimator of the same probabilities (for example fitting the per-game margin distribution) would need fewer seeds but is a different estimator and would need its own bake-off; not proposed here.
- Limits: one run (adopted stack, fold 2), blocks of up to 100 seeds measured and larger k extrapolated by the binomial law; close lines only (the live publish uses the line at run time); seeds are independent by construction, so no between-seed correlation is assumed beyond what the run itself shows.
