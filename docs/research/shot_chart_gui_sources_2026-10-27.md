# Shot-chart GUI sources for NCAA men's basketball (desk survey, 2026-10-27)

Worker: Sonnet. Brief: can API Anything enlarge the shot-coordinate dataset via a vendor other than ESPN? Desk survey with light probing; no api-anything operations, captures or pulls were created. Nothing on the do-not-scrape list was touched. Follow-up to `docs/research/shot_coordinate_sources_2026-10-27.md`.

## Facts for the PM (no recommendations)

1. **A second, independent coordinate dataset already exists as static files: `JackLich10/gamezoneR-data` on GitHub** (`data/play_by_play/rds/pbp_<season>.rds`, 16 files, 2008-09 to 2023-24; repo last pushed 2024-03-11; GitHub reports no repo licence; the gamezoneR code is Apache-2.0). It is a third party's scrape of STATS LLC (now Stats Perform) GameZone. Reading it needs no API Anything: plain raw.githubusercontent.com download.
2. **Probed (6 files downloaded to the session scratchpad, read with pyreadr under `python -I`, nothing saved in the repo):** columns include `game_id, date, home, away, home_name, away_name, neutral, shot_outcome, free_throw, three_pt, shot_desc, loc_x, loc_y, shooter_id, home_1..5, away_1..5`. Shot rows (non-free-throw) are 99.99-100% located in every probed file, in every game (every game >50% located; zero games with zero located). Per file:

   | file | games | shots | located % | date range |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 2014-15 | 2,195 | 241,108 | 100.0 | 2013-12-27 to 2015-04-06 |
   | 2016-17 | 2,147 | 248,279 | 100.0 | 2013-12-27 to 2017-04-03 |
   | 2018-19 | 2,205 | 255,276 | 100.0 | 2018-11-06 to 2019-04-08 |
   | 2020-21 | 1,620 | 188,448 | 100.0 | 2020-11-25 to 2021-04-05 |
   | 2022-23 | 2,235 | 258,945 | 100.0 | 2022-11-07 to 2023-04-03 |
   | 2023-24 | 1,294 | 153,823 | 100.0 | 2023-11-06 to 2024-01-15 (feed stops mid-January) |

   The 2014-15 and 2016-17 files contain a minimum date of 2013-12-27, which looks wrong for those seasons (possible mislabelled or mixed-season rows; not investigated). Not downloaded: 2008-09 to 2013-14, 2015-16, 2017-18, 2019-20, 2021-22. Whether the earliest files carry real coordinates is untested (the package README says "dating back to the 2017-18 season").
3. **Coordinate frame:** `loc_x` 0 to about 50, `loc_y` 0 to about 47 (feet, half-court frame, basket-relative); differs from our CBBD tenths-of-feet 940 x 500 frame and from hoopR's. A conversion and a frame check would be required.
4. **Coverage is partial at the game level:** about 2,200 games per full season versus 5,749 D1 regular + postseason games in our 2022-23 universe (about 39%). Matched on date + team names (either orientation) against `data/processed/games_universe_v2.parquet`, 2022-23: 1,945 of 2,235 GameZone games matched (the 290 unmatched were not investigated; name-format mismatches are visible in the sample, e.g. "Saint Francis U", "Maryland Eastern Shore").
5. **Overlap with our existing gaps, 2022-23 only** (our "located" = more than 50% of FGA located in CBBD or hoopR, per game): our unlocated D1 games 392 of 5,749 (6.8%); of those, 73 are in GameZone. Neutral-site or postseason games unlocated in ours: 111; of those 39 are in GameZone. GameZone covers 90.3% of postseason (season_type 3) games and 49.4% of neutral-site games in our universe, versus 33.8% of all games. Other seasons not computed; for 2019-2021, where our located shares are low (CBBD sample 28-71%, hoopR 11-14%), the overlap was not measured.
6. **GameZone provenance and liveness:** the data originates from `api.gamezone.stats.com/Basketball/Service.svc/league/cbk/events/<id>` (per gamezoneR source). DNS for `gamezone.stats.com` and `api.gamezone.stats.com` returns NXDOMAIN today (2026-10-27); a gamezoneR GitHub issue dated 2024-11-18 is titled "Package Broken?" and says the core is broken with "the stats api transitioning". Package last pushed 2023-04-11; data bot last pushed 2024-03-11. The live vendor feed is gone; only the frozen GitHub copy remains, ending 2024-01-15. Nothing for 2023-24 after mid-January, 2024-25 or 2025-26.
7. **GameZone terms:** no licence file in the data repo; no terms found on any Stats Perform page my tools could read (statsperform.com returns 403 to curl and WebFetch; a third-party mirror excerpt mentions only the right to deny access). The data is STATS LLC content republished by a third party without a stated licence. Not on the CLAUDE.md do-not-scrape list. Terms status: unresolved.
8. **ESPN game-page shot chart is the same feed as the summary endpoint** (one probe, game 401408636, 2022 national semifinal): the page embeds `"shtChrt":{"athlts","plays",...}`; its 175 plays with a `coordinate` all carried the same play id and identical x/y as the 361-play `site.api.espn.com .../summary?event=` response (175 of 175 identical). Nothing new.
9. **Sidearm school sites did not expose Genius shot charts in the probe:** 1 box-score page (goduke.com, Duke vs Siena 2026-03-19; page flags `isFromStatcrew`/`isFromDakstats`) and its one linked PDF box score (8 pages, text-only, zero embedded images, no shot-chart text). The Duke page is a Sidearm Nextgen page fed by a StatCrew export, not a Genius LiveStats page. Duke's robots.txt (`User-agent: *`) disallows `/services/`, `/common/`, `/documents/`, `/site/`, `/admin/`, `/images/`. The Sidearm terms page (sidearmsports.com/terms) is JS-rendered and returned no readable text.
10. **Genius Sports NCAA LiveStats** is the NCAA's official stats system (partnership extended April 2025 through 2032 per press release); its public viewer lives on NCAA surfaces, which are out under the NCAA's September 2026 terms (previous survey, fact 8). I found no evidence that school Sidearm sites republish Genius shot x/y. Genius website terms were not located (guessed URLs 404; the privacy page has no scraping clause).
11. **StatBroadcast:** every fetch (statbroadcast.com, /terms, /support, the blog post) returned 403 to curl and WebFetch. The only shot-chart evidence is StatBroadcast's "ShotChartr" tool (2012 and 2015 blog posts, via search snippets): a web app for a team's stat crew to keep a computerized shot chart and export PDFs after each half or game. Duke's page links a StatMonitr live view (`statbroadcast.com/events/statmonitr.php?gid=duke`). I found no evidence of a public per-game shot-chart tab or an XML with x/y.
12. **Media gamecenters:** CBS Sports college basketball statistical data is supplied by Genius Sports (sportsvideo.org, 2024-02-02, via search); no confirmation of 2025-26 gametracker shot charts. Yahoo and NCAA.com: vendor and shot-chart presence not confirmed (searches returned nothing; NCAA.com `/terms-use` and a guessed `data.ncaa.com` scoreboard path returned 404). FOX and Yahoo terms prohibit automated collection (quotes in the table).
13. **Side fact about our primary source:** ESPN's robots.txt disallows GPTBot, CCBot, ChatGPT-User, anthropic-ai and others at `/`. The Disney Terms of Use (https://disneytermsofuse.com/english/) prohibit extracting Disney Products "using a robot, spider, script, or other automated means, including, for the avoidance of doubt, for the purposes of creating or developing any AI Tool, data mining or web scraping or otherwise compiling, building, creating or contributing to any collection of data, data set or database". CLAUDE.md treats hoopR (CC BY 4.0) as primary and does not list ESPN's own site; the reach of these clauses over hoopR's redistribution was not assessed.
