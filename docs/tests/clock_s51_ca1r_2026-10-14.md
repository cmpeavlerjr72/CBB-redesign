## Reading (evidence only)

- The role split moves the leader lines toward actual against CA1E without closing the gap beyond floor on F2:
  - team-prior leader: CA1E -0.0131, CA1R +0.0016, actual +0.0192.
  - wc z_off leader: -1.23 vs -1.01, actual -0.30.
  - wc strength leader: -0.79 vs -0.49, actual -0.21.
- Both arms impose a within-cell leader response larger than the 2024-25 realisation. CA1R also sharpens the trailer lines:
  - wc z_off trailer -1.28 vs actual -1.51.
  - wc strength trailer -0.80 vs actual -0.63.
  - team-prior trailer -0.038 vs actual -0.035.
- The CA1R training fit gives a resolved negative leader z_off coefficient on both folds (about 8 to 13 SE). That is the source of the leader-line overshoot on the test season. The served law and CA0 sit near zero on those lines.
- Defence-z (pooled quintile slope) fails for both arms, slightly worse for CA1R (+0.0214 vs actual +0.0064, control +0.0087). Entering the role defence terms did not fix that line; the pooled within-cell defence-z (reported) also stays short of actual.
- The verdict does not depend on the tied-role lines (all pass) or on G1 SD (reported only).
