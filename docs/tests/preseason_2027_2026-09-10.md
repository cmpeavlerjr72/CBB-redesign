## 2. Roster continuity, 2026-27 vs 2025-26 (`scripts/build_roster_continuity.py`)

### Method and its limits (read before trusting the numbers)

Because CBBD's 2027 roster is empty (section 1), "returning" cannot be observed
directly -- it is inferred: a player who appears in hoopR `player_box_2026` for a
team and does **not** appear in the transfer portal (`year=2026`) as having left
that team is counted as returning. This is a **one-sided signal**: it misses
graduated seniors, early draft entrants, and medical retirements, none of which
show up in the transfer portal. **Returning shares below are therefore an upper
bound on true returning shares** -- tightest for teams with few departing
seniors, loosest for senior-heavy teams that lost their core to graduation/the
draft rather than the portal. This will self-correct once CBBD populates real
2027 rosters; treat these numbers as a preseason planning input, not a final
truth table.

Portal players are matched to hoopR `player_box_2026` athlete ids in three tiers
(mirroring `src/cbb_sim/data/player_ids.py`'s own CBBD<->ESPN ladder):
1. the existing `data/processed/player_crosswalk.parquet` (CBBD<->ESPN player
   crosswalk keyed on name + team, >=99% source-id-verified for season 2026);
2. the raw `data/raw/cbbd/rosters/roster_2026.parquet` dump's own `source_id`;
3. direct normalized-name + team match against hoopR `player_box_2026` (no
   jersey number available from the portal payload, so "name + team" only, not
   "name + team + jersey").
Ambiguous same-team name collisions are dropped rather than guessed at at every
tier (same policy as `player_ids._unique_key_map`).

**Match rates**, departures (portal rows whose origin team is a D-I-ever
crosswalk team): **96.8%** (1,502 via the crosswalk, 2 via direct name+team,
49 unmatched out of 1,553). Incoming transfers (destination side): **95.7%**
(1,230 via the crosswalk, 2 via direct name+team, 56 unmatched out of 1,288).
Both comfortably clear a "trustworthy for aggregate shares" bar; the unmatched
tail is concentrated in name-formatting mismatches (suffixes, nicknames) the
normalizer doesn't catch and in players who left too early/late to have a
`player_box_2026` row at all.

Freshman count = CBBD's ranked recruiting board (`/recruiting/players?year=2026`)
committed to that team -- **a lower bound**: 396 ranked prospects nationally as
of the pull date is far short of a true ~700-1,400-player incoming freshman
class across 365 D-I teams, because CBBD's board only tracks scouted/ranked
signees. Median freshman count per team in this table is **0**; read it as "at
least this many," not "exactly this many."
