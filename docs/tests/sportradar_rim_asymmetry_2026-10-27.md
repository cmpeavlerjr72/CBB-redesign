# Sportradar rim-placement asymmetry (2026-10-27)

Research-only data (user ruling 2026-10-27; never synced to HF or rfq-collab). Diagnostic; nothing is fit or adopted. Script `scripts/diag_sportradar_rim_v1.py` (loader and ESPN match from `scripts/diag_sportradar_inventory_v1.py`, inventory in `docs/tests/sportradar_inventory_2026-10-27.md`); tables are also in `docs/tests/sportradar_rim_asymmetry_2026-10-27.json`. It repeats the rim measurements of `docs/tests/coordinate_audit_2026-10-27.md` (ESPN/CBBD) on an independent vendor.

## Scope, definitions, floors

- **Seasons.** Sportradar season 2013 = 2013-14 ... 2017 = 2017-18. Season 2013 carries no `shot_type` and its descriptions say only 'two point shot', so layups, dunks and tips cannot be identified: every rim measurement is NOT DEFINED for 2013 (it appears only in the distance-agreement table, with ESPN's shot type). No outcome data from the sealed 2025-26 season is used.

- **Rim class (mapping).** layup = `shot_type == 'layup'` or description contains 'layup'; dunk = `shot_type == 'dunk'` or 'dunk'; tip = `shot_type == 'tip shot'` or 'tip shot'. `hook shot` is NOT rim (it stays in jump2, as ESPN's JumpShot does). `shot_subtype` (2016-17 only: driving, putback, alley-oop, finger roll...) is not used for the class; it is a modifier of layup / dunk / jump shot. The ESPN side uses `type_text` LayUpShot / DunkShot / TipShot.

- **Frame and distance.** Inches; court folded onto one half (x > 564 -> 1128 - x); distance = feet from the textbook basket (63 in from the baseline, 300 in across), dunk median distance 2.4-3.0 ft per season (inventory doc). ESPN distance = sqrt(raw_y^2 + y^2) (basket-relative frame, as in the audit). Section (a) also reports the 6-ft rate with the basket moved to each season's dunk centroid (sensitivity).

- **Floors.** Paired comparisons are within game, weights n_a n_b / (n_a + n_b), floor = SD over 300 bootstrap resamples of games (200 for pixel rows), exactly `paired_diff` from `scripts/diag_coordinate_audit_v1.py`. Cells under 500 attempts in either group are flagged UNDERPOWERED and not read.

- **Reference (ESPN/CBBD, 2022-2025 audit).** Layups/tips charted more than 6 ft from the rim: 6-7% of makes vs 15-16% of misses, within-game gap -8.4 to -8.9 pts, floor 0.2; rim makes 1.3-2.1 pts less likely to carry a coordinate; one spike pixel at the basket making 2.3-3.2 pts less than its ring.

## Verdict

**Does an independent vendor show the same outcome-dependent rim charting as ESPN? Yes for the placement asymmetry, and it cannot be tested for the other two symptoms.** Sportradar charts layups, dunks and tips more than 6 ft from the rim far more often when the shot missed: 13.6 / 5.2 / 8.2 / 6.6% of makes against 25.3 / 13.7 / 18.8 / 16.5% of misses (2014-15 to 2017-18), a within-game gap of -11.1 / -8.3 / -10.4 / -9.7 points against a bootstrap floor of 0.25-0.30 (z -34 to -40), the same sign and about the same size as the ESPN/CBBD -8.4 to -8.9 points of 2022-25. It survives moving the basket to each season's dunk centroid (-4.2 to -5.7 points, floor 0.15-0.21, z -27 to -29), holds for layups and, smaller, for dunks (-2.8 to -6.2 points) and tips (-2.7 to -3.7), and make rate falls smoothly with distance (79-82% inside 1 ft to 36-43% beyond 6 ft). The two vendors are genuinely independent (on 72-108k joined plays both locate per season their positions differ by a median of 2.0-2.4 ft for rim plays and only 36-41% of rim distances agree within 1 ft), and rim MISSES are the less consistent placements: 32-37% of miss distances agree within 1 ft against 39-45% for makes, and 17.8-25.4% of misses differ by more than 3 ft against 9.9-17.6% of makes. The sharpest test conditions on ESPN: among rim plays ESPN puts within 3 ft of the rim (ESPN puts 99.5-99.9% of layups within 6 ft), Sportradar places the same play more than 6 ft away for 3.8-10.7% of makes but 8.8-17.1% of misses (gap -5.2 to -9.3 points, floor 0.4-0.65), so the asymmetry is not just misses being truly farther out. Two things are not reproduced. (b) Sportradar carries a coordinate on every attempt (0 of 92-108k rim attempts lack one, no (0,0) or sentinel rows), so the outcome-dependent omission of makes cannot occur and cannot be tested; ESPN's own 2014-18 files lose 1.2-2.3 points more makes than misses from the located set pooled, but that is between games (located-or-not is decided per game there, within-game gap 0). (c) There is no placeholder pixel: the largest 1-inch pixel holds 0.07-0.09% of rim attempts (50-94 attempts, UNDERPOWERED, neighbour ratio at most 5), and the largest 6-inch cell holds 1.0-1.3% with a neighbour ratio at most 1.4 and make-rate differences from its ring of -4.3 to +3.5 points against floors of 1.4-2.3. Caveats that matter: the ESPN 2014-18 vintage on the SAME games is nearly clean (rim >6 ft, 2014-15 to 2017-18: 0.06-0.24% of makes vs 0.25-0.52% of misses, gap -0.2 to -0.3 points, floor 0.04-0.06), so outcome-dependent charting is a property of a vendor and vintage, not of the sport; its integer-foot grid puts 6-7% of rim attempts on the basket pixel, which makes 3.5-5.9 points less than its ring in every season (floor about 1.2-1.4), the same direction as the 2022-25 spike pixel but on a grid too coarse to separate from geometry; Sportradar is noisier than ESPN in general (dunk median distance 2.4-3.0 ft from the textbook basket, with a 0.5-1 ft frame offset that moves between 2014-15 and 2015-16), and I cannot tell from these data whether Sportradar's charter sees the outcome or misses are simply harder to place. Season 2013-14 has no shot type, so the rim measurements are not defined there. Net: a second, independent vendor shows the same pattern, so rim x/y should still not be used as an outcome-blind input.
