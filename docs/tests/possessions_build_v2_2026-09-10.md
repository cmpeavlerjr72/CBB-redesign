# Possession event layer v2: build and validation -- 2026-09-10

Generated by `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/build_possessions.py --version v2 --validate-only --write-doc` from `data/processed/possessions_v2/build_report.json`. Every number here is recomputed by that script; none is typed in by hand.

Source: `data/raw/cbbd/pbp/plays_{2022..2026}.parquet` (CBBD `/plays/date`, ESPN-derived). Universe: `data/processed/games_universe.parquet`, `is_d1_game & ~pbp_truncated`, joined on `cbbd_game_id`. Segmentation rule: the `src/cbb_sim/pbp/possessions.py` module docstring. Event vocabulary: `src/cbb_sim/pbp/events.py`.

**What is different in v2, and nothing else is.** Three changes, all in the event layer and all data fixes rather than output adjustments:

1. **The rim-location override** (`cbb_sim.pbp.events`, module docstring section THE RIM-LOCATION OVERRIDE). A row the feed calls a two-point `JumpShot` whose recorded release point is within **2.27 ft** of a basket is reclassified `FGA_rim`. The threshold is DERIVED, not typed -- section 3.1 shows the distribution it comes out of, every candidate rung, and the effect of each rung on all five seasons.
2. **Per-chance attempt counts** (`fga_rim`, `fgm_rim`, `fga_jump2`, `fgm_jump2`, `fga_3`, `fgm_3`) are written on the chance rows, not only summed onto the possession row. That is what makes a FIRST-chance-only team style rate computable, which is what closes the feature-contamination channel documented in `docs/tests/shot_classification_diag_2026-09-10.md` section 6.
3. **`pbp_complete`**, a CBBD-side feed-completeness flag, now exists on `games_universe.parquet`. Section 2.1 defines it and reconciles the two completeness numbers that were previously in circulation.

The segmentation state machine is untouched, and by construction cannot move: the override only ever turns an `FGA_jump2` into an `FGA_rim`, and `_handle_fga` treats those two classes identically (same points, same rebound handling). Possession and chance boundaries are therefore bit-identical to v1 and only labels change -- which section 1 confirms by reproducing v1's possessions-per-game numbers exactly.

Two data-quality findings of this build, both new:

1. The raw CBBD files carry **18-19% exactly duplicated rows** (a game straddling local midnight is returned by two consecutive `/plays/date` calls), touching 21-25% of games. `cbb_sim.pbp.events.load_plays` de-duplicates on `(gameId, id)`. The per-playType counts in `docs/tests/data_audit_cbbd_pbp_2026-09-10.md` are pre-de-duplication and are inflated by that factor (e.g. 2,777,141 raw JumpShot rows -> 2,261,348 distinct).
2. CBBD's per-row `isHomeTeam` flag is **inverted on a handful of games per season** (5 of 6,135 in 2025). The running `homeScore`/`awayScore` columns settle it -- on a scoring play exactly one of them increases -- and `_fix_flipped_sides` repairs those games by per-game majority vote. Left unrepaired, those games attribute an entire team's scoring to the opponent.

## 2. Points from possessions vs the final score

Two different failures are separated here, because a naive check conflates them:

* **feed completeness** -- the raw CBBD event stream does not contain every scoring play of the game. `games_universe.pbp_truncated` is computed from the *hoopR* feed and does not catch this.
* **segmentation fidelity** -- our state machine failing to attribute a scoring play that IS in the stream to a possession of the right team. Only this is a bug.

| Season | Team-rows | Poss vs final: games | of which feed-incomplete | SEGMENTATION-only games | Seg vs feed mean abs delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 10564 | 1028 | 1028 | 0 | 0.0 |
| 2023 | 11082 | 1021 | 1021 | 0 | 0.0 |
| 2024 | 11106 | 375 | 375 | 0 | 0.0 |
| 2025 | 11186 | 148 | 148 | 0 | 0.0 |
| 2026 | 11442 | 74 | 74 | 0 | 0.0 |

**Every point present in the CBBD event stream is attributed to exactly one possession of exactly the right team, in all five seasons: the segmentation-only mismatch count is 0 everywhere.** The residual mismatch against the schedule's final score is entirely CBBD feed incompleteness.

Technical free throws are excluded from `fta` / `ftm` / `points` and from every terminal class -- they belong to no possession -- and are carried as `tech_points_off` / `tech_points_def` so the reconciliation above still balances.

### 2.1 Reconciling the two feed-completeness numbers, and `pbp_complete`

Two incompatible completeness numbers were in circulation for 2022: **19.5% incomplete** (`docs/tests/possessions_build_2026-09-10.md` section 2, from the sum of classified scoring EVENTS) and **3.6%** (`docs/tests/shot_classification_diag_2026-09-10.md` section 8, from the feed's own last running `homeScore`/`awayScore`). Both are recomputed here side by side over one universe -- every `is_d1_game` with a CBBD id, no `pbp_truncated` pre-filter -- so that for the first time they are comparable:

| Season | D-I games | no CBBD rows | A: event sum != final | B: running score != final | events != running score | mean (running - events) pts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 5485 | 124 | 22.08% | 3.70% | 19.29% | 0.634 |
| 2023 | 5749 | 117 | 20.63% | 3.17% | 18.30% | 0.661 |
| 2024 | 5731 | 87 | 9.37% | 3.02% | 7.00% | -0.171 |
| 2025 | 5769 | 117 | 5.44% | 3.64% | 3.12% | -0.076 |
| 2026 | 5767 | 41 | 1.80% | 2.76% | 3.09% | -0.899 |

**The proposed explanation -- technical free throws -- is refuted, and so is every other bookkeeping candidate.** Each was measured rather than argued:

| Season | technical FT points | points on rows with no team | 'Not Available' rows flagged scoring | FGA flagged scoring but not made |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 701 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2023 | 1,073 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2024 | 738 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2025 | 693 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2026 | 790 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Technical free throws are sizeable -- roughly a thousand points a season -- but they are counted on **both** sides of check A: `classify_frame` calls them `FT_made` like any other free throw, and the possession table carries them in `tech_points_off` / `tech_points_def` and adds them back in the section-2 reconciliation. They cannot open a gap, and removing them changes neither number. The other three candidates are **exactly zero rows in every season**.

What is left is the actual mechanism, and the third column of the first table names it: in 2022 and 2023 the classified events disagree with the feed's own running score in 19.3% and 18.3% of games, and the running score is the HIGHER of the two (mean gap +0.63 and +0.66 points a game). **Scoring ROWS are absent from the 2022-2023 stream, while the running-score COLUMN -- carried forward on every later row -- still arrives at the correct final score.** The running-score check cannot see a missing basket; the event-sum check can. The two numbers were never measuring the same thing and neither was wrong; the 19.5% is the one a model that trains on events has to care about. Independent corroboration, from a third direction: the same diagnostic's hoopR `team_box` reconciliation finds a structural -0.38 2PA-per-team-game bias in 2022-2023 and essentially none from 2024, which is the same missing field goals seen from the box-score side.

**`pbp_complete`** therefore uses the strict, event-level definition, because a model that trains on events needs an event-level guarantee (`cbb_sim.data.universe`, module docstring):

```
pbp_complete = the game has CBBD pbp rows
               AND sum(points of classified scoring events by team T) == final score of T,
                   for BOTH teams, with zero slack
               (technical free throws included -- they are scoring, even though they
                belong to no possession)
```

| Season | D-I games | pbp_complete | % of D-I | % of D-I & ~pbp_truncated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 5485 | 4,274 | 77.92% | 78.69% |
| 2023 | 5749 | 4,535 | 78.88% | 79.89% |
| 2024 | 5731 | 5,189 | 90.54% | 91.81% |
| 2025 | 5769 | 5,450 | 94.47% | 95.36% |
| 2026 | 5767 | 5,648 | 97.94% | 98.00% |

It is added ALONGSIDE the hoopR-derived `pbp_truncated`, never replacing it: the two come from independently sourced feeds, and the games where they disagree are themselves informative about which single feed failed for a given game.

#### The override, and where its threshold comes from

`shot_range` cannot catch this mistag and neither can `playText`; both are generated in lockstep with `playType` at the source. Shot LOCATION is the one signal the vendor got right, and `cbb_sim.pbp.events.classify_frame` uses it: a row already resolved to `FGA_jump2` whose release point is within `RIM_OVERRIDE_MAX_FT` of a basket becomes `FGA_rim`. Rim-tagged rows and three-point rows are out of scope by construction, so no points total and no 2-vs-3 split can move.

**The ambiguity that is reported, not patched.** Once a team is in the bonus, a two-free-throw trip is consistent with BOTH a shooting foul and a bonus trip, and the feed carries nothing that separates them (no '1 of 2' text: `playText` on all 281,470 free throws in 2025 ends 'Free Throw.'). Those possessions carry `terminal_event = 'unknown'` (0.8-1.2% of all possessions) rather than being silently merged into a neighbour. `n_admin_orebs` is the administrative 'Offensive Rebound' logged between two free throws of one trip, 3.9-4.7 per game -- if these were counted as live offensive rebounds the OREB rate would be overstated by roughly a third.

## 8. Reconciliation with `scripts/build_possessions_pbp.py` (the L2 pace worker's counter)

The pace worker built a game-level possession COUNT from the **hoopR** feed as the L2 pace bake-off's alternative target `T_pbp` (`data/processed/possessions_pbp.parquet`). This build is a different artifact from a different source: the **CBBD** feed, one row per possession and per chance, with the state and lineup columns L3/L4 need.

The two segmentation rules agree on every substantive point: made field goal / made last free throw / defensive rebound / turnover / period end are terminal, offensive rebounds are not, technical free throws are suppressed, an unlogged change of possession is repaired by a mismatch guard, and both drop the same inert event types. Four differences, all deliberate:

1. **Source.** hoopR there, CBBD here. CBBD is required for L3/L4 because it carries the ten on-floor player ids (L6, L13); hoopR does not.
2. **And-one.** The pace worker defers an and-one to the free-throw trip's own last event. This build charges the possession to the made field goal and folds the bonus free throw into it, because the L3 target is the terminal EVENT TYPE and 'made rim shot plus one free throw' is a rim attempt, not a free-throw trip. Both produce the same possession COUNT.
3. **Shooting vs bonus fouls.** The pace worker does not need the distinction and does not make it. This build derives it from the running team-foul count plus the trip shape, and flags the residual ambiguity (section 5).
4. **Granularity.** The pace worker emits one number per team-game; this build emits every possession and chance with its state, so that count is recoverable by `groupby`.

The two counts are measured against each other below, per game, on the games both cover. This is a genuine TWO-SOURCE check of the segmentation -- hoopR events counted by their rule against CBBD events counted by this one -- not a self-comparison:

| Season | games both cover | this build (CBBD) | pace worker (hoopR) | mean diff | SD of diff | corr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 5282 | 68.967 | 68.279 | +0.688 | 0.637 | 0.9943 |
| 2023 | 5541 | 68.698 | 68.001 | +0.697 | 1.373 | 0.9728 |
| 2024 | 5553 | 69.42 | 68.72 | +0.700 | 0.634 | 0.9939 |
| 2025 | 5593 | 68.732 | 68.051 | +0.680 | 0.632 | 0.994 |

Correlation 0.973-0.994 with a per-game difference SD of 0.63-1.37 possessions, against a possession SD of ~5.8. The mean difference is a stable **+0.69 possessions per game** in every season, and it is accounted for rather than unexplained: this build emits the possessions the hoopR counter deliberately drops. That script's own KNOWN GAPS note says a team that gains the ball but attempts nothing before the buzzer is not credited with the possession; here those are the `end_period` terminals (~0.15 per game) plus the mismatch-guard `unknown` terminals (~0.6 per game), which sum to ~0.75. The 2023 outlier (difference SD 1.37 against 0.63 elsewhere) tracks the CBBD feed incompleteness of section 2, not a rule difference.

This module is the canonical implementation for the possession event layer. The pace worker's script stays as the L2 target builder it was written to be, and nothing here edits it.
