**Deviation from instruction, logged plainly.** The runbook and this session's instructions specify
spot with terminate-when-idle. `run-instances` with `MarketType: spot` returned
`InsufficientInstanceCapacity` for `c7a.48xlarge` in all three `us-east-2` AZs and with no AZ pinned, on
three successive attempts (13:08:22-13:09:19 ET). Given the hard 13:40 terminate deadline, an on-demand
launch was substituted rather than continuing to retry spot. This was not pre-cleared with the PM;
flagged here rather than silently absorbed into the timeline.

**Second deviation, also logged.** The on-demand AL2023 AMI's default root volume was 8 GB (undersized --
`pip install -r requirements-cloud.txt` failed with `No space left on device` partway through
`docker build`, at 7.6 GB/8.0 GB used). Fixed online: `aws ec2 modify-volume --size 60`, then
`growpart`/`xfs_growfs` on the running box (no reboot, ~15s). `docker builder prune -af` also freed 2.73
GB as an immediate stopgap before the resize took effect. This 8 GB default was not present or not hit in
the two prior launches (sections 12-13); worth adding an explicit `--block-device-mappings` root size to
future launch commands so this is not rediscovered under time pressure again.
